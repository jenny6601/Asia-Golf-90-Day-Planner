# Phase 9.4 — Fuji Five Lakes / Kawaguchiko golf course catalogue

## Scope
Six courses around Lake Kawaguchi and the Fuji Five Lakes area have been added to the Japan catalogue. Their names and addresses are supported by Yamanashi tourism and/or prefectural government listings. GPS coordinates have deliberately been left null until verified against authoritative course maps.

## Readiness
All six records have coordinateStatus pending, null latitude/longitude and provider availability pending. They can be displayed in the catalogue, but the current UI disables forecast selection until GPS verification. This is intentional and prevents address-derived or guessed coordinates from entering forecast scoring.
